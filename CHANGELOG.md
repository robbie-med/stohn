# Changelog

Evidence and rule changes are listed with the date they were reviewed.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-28
- Redesign: editorial layout without cards, bundled Fraunces + Gowun Batang, view transitions,
  bottom-sheet safety dialogs.

  Danger-sign alerts from check-ins still appear.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-28 (evidence 2026.09.28)
- First release: explainer, daily check-in with escalation-only rules, visit prep,
  printable summary, evidence page. English and Korean.
- Evidence verified against PubMed abstracts on 2026-09-28. Deviations from the project brief:
  - Time-to-passage figures use Miller & Kane 1999 (J Urol) directly: ≤2 mm 8.2 d / 4.8 % intervention;
    2–4 mm 12.2 d / 17 %; 4–6 mm 22.1 d / 50 %. The brief's "UW teaching slide" figures
    (3 % / 14 % / 99 % for > 6 mm) are not used.
  - ROKS external validation cites Iremashvili 2019 (AUC 0.655 at 2 y, 0.605 at 5 y).
  - PUSH trial (Desai et al., Lancet 2026): 19 % vs 20 % symptomatic events, HR 0.96.
- AUA 2026 statements (surgical: 7–9, 38; medical: 2.5 L urine target) are summarised from
  secondary sources. Re-check them against the full text.
